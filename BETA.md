# Private beta gates

Rollout stages: internal accounts, 10 trusted testers, 25 users, then at most 100 users.

Advance only when the prior stage has no high-severity security or data-integrity issue. Track authentication completion, portfolio connection/import completion, time to first briefing, weekly briefing views, research follow-through, connection failures, and AI cost. Never send balances, holdings, transactions, emails, or prompts to analytics.

Success targets: 70% authentication completion, 50% portfolio connection/import, median first briefing under three minutes, and 35% of activated users returning in at least three of their first four weeks.
